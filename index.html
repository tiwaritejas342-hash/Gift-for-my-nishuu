I'm Really Sorry, Tanishi

Take your time reading this... no rush, no arguments.

Kandhon tak aati teri woh chhoti si zulfon ki ladi,
Aur teri woh pyaari si muskaan, jo aaj kahin kho si gayi hai.
Jaanta hoon mere saath hokar bhi tujhe meri kami si mehsoos hoti hai,
Aur andar hi andar tu khud ko le kar thodi insecure si rehti hai.
Kuch purane zakhm hain tere kal ke, jo abhi tak bhare nahi,
Aur kuch meri galtiyon ke nishaan, jo abhi tak mitaaye gaye nahi.
Par main is har kami ko bharna chahta hoon, khud ko sach mein badal kar,
Tujhe woh saara pyaar dena chahta hoon, har ek pal mein dhal kar.
Bas ek baar mera haath thaam kar mere saamne baith ja,
Apne dil ki har uljhan, har darr mujhse khul kar keh ja.
Main koi behas nahi karunga, bas teri har baat sunna chahta hoon,
Teri us khoyi hui muskaan ko phir se wapas laana chahta hoon.

Mera Vaada Tumse, Tanishi 🤝
